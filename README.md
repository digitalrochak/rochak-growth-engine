# Rochak's Growth Hub

Create a modern, premium, professional personal portfolio website for Rochak Regmi, a professional Digital Marketer specializing in paid advertising, lead generation, graphic design, and Local SEO.

1. Personal Profile / Introduction

Rochak Regmi is a professional Digital Marketer who helps businesses grow through strategic digital marketing services. His core expertise includes Facebook & Instagram Ads, Google Ads, Lead Generation, Graphic Design, and Local SEO.

The website should immediately communicate that Rochak is a results-focused digital marketing professional who helps businesses generate leads, increase sales, build brand awareness, improve online visibility, and reach the right customers.

Include a professional profile picture of Rochak prominently in the hero section. The profile image should have a clean, polished, professional appearance and work naturally with the overall visual design.

Suggested hero messaging:

Headline:
"Digital Marketing That Drives Real Business Growth"

Supporting text:
"Helping businesses generate leads, increase sales, build brand awareness, and grow their online presence through strategic digital marketing."

Include strong calls-to-action such as:

Work With Me

Get Started

View My Services

Build the website with a clean, modern one-page portfolio structure containing:

 Footer

Do not include an Education section because Rochak does not want his educational background displayed.

" I am a professional Digital Marketer specializing in Facebook & Instagram Ads, Google Ads, Lead Generation, Graphic Design, and Local SEO."

Expand this into polished website copy that communicates Rochak's focus on helping businesses attract customers, generate leads, increase sales, build awareness, and improve their online presence.

Use a professional portrait or profile image alongside the text.

Avoid unnecessary personal information and keep the section focused on professional expertise.
